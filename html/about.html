WHO WE ARE

A little guidance can open up a world of possibilities.

Career Compass AI is a career exploration platform designed to make the process of discovering career options more approachable and organized.

Through a simple assessment, the platform considers your responses and presents career suggestions connected to different interests and strengths.

Our goal is to help students begin exploring possibilities with more clarity and confidence.
