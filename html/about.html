✦ About Career Compass AI

Your journey to a better career.

Career Compass AI is designed to help students understand their interests, discover their strengths, and explore career paths that match who they are.

Career decisions should feel easier.

Choosing a career can sometimes feel confusing. There are hundreds of possibilities, different courses, skills and opportunities to consider.

Career Compass AI brings these pieces together into one simple experience where you can explore your interests and understand possible career directions.

Our goal is not to choose your future for you. It is to help you understand your options so you can make more confident decisions.
