What is Career Compass AI?

Career Compass AI is a student-focused career exploration platform that brings career discovery, self-assessment and career information together in one place.

Instead of choosing a career based only on trends or suggestions from others, students can explore different possibilities and learn more about what each career involves.

The goal is simple: help you understand your options before making important decisions about your future.
