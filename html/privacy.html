Privacy Policy

We believe that exploring your future should feel safe and transparent. This page explains how information may be handled when you use Career Compass AI.

About This Policy

Career Compass AI is currently a developing educational project. The website may store some information in your browser to support features such as assessment results and saved careers. This draft describes the intended handling of information for the current demo and should be reviewed and updated as the website evolves.

On This Page

1. Overview

Career Compass AI is designed to help users explore possible career paths, reflect on their interests and learn about different opportunities.

This policy describes the types of information that may be entered or stored while using the current website and explains the choices available to users.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on which features are used, the website may handle information that a user enters.

Profile Information

Users may enter details such as their name, educational interests or other profile information in relevant forms.

Assessment Responses

Answers entered into the career questionnaire may be used to display assessment outcomes and career suggestions.

Saved Careers

If users save careers, the website may store the selected career details in browser storage so that they can be shown again later.

Contact Form Information

The contact form may ask for a name, email address, subject and message. In the current demo, submitting the form displays a local confirmation only; it does not deliver the message to a team inbox.

3. How Information Is Used

Information entered into Career Compass AI may be used for the following purposes, depending on the features enabled:

  • To display profile details entered by the user.
  • To process assessment responses and show results.
  • To display and manage saved career options.
  • To support navigation between website pages.
  • To improve the website during development and testing.

Career suggestions are intended to support exploration and should not be treated as a guaranteed outcome or a substitute for personal research and guidance.

4. Data Storage

Some current website features use the browser's localStorage to retain information, such as assessment responses or saved careers, on the same browser and device.

Browser storage is not the same as a secure server database. Information saved there may remain on the device until it is removed by the website, cleared through browser settings or otherwise deleted.

If a backend or external database is added in the future, this section must be updated to explain how that information is stored and managed.

5. Sharing of Information

The current static demo does not include a configured backend for sending profile or assessment information to a Career Compass AI server. However, external resources used by the page, such as web fonts, may make requests to their respective providers when loaded.

If external services, analytics, hosting integrations or a backend are added later, this policy should clearly identify what information is shared, with whom and why.

6. Security

We aim to design the website with care. However, information kept in browser storage does not provide the same protection as properly secured server-side storage.

Avoid entering passwords, financial details, highly sensitive personal information or other private information into this demo.

Before the website is used with real user accounts or sensitive information, suitable security measures and a review of the implementation will be necessary.

7. Your Choices

Users can manage information stored by the current demo through available page features and their browser settings.

  • Remove saved careers using the Saved Careers page.
  • Clear browser site data through browser settings.
  • Choose not to enter optional information into forms.
  • Stop using the website at any time.

Clearing browser storage may remove saved careers, assessment answers or other locally stored information used by the website.

8. Young Users

Career Compass AI may be used by students exploring educational and career options. Users should avoid submitting unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

If the project is later made available to younger users at scale, its data collection, consent processes and privacy practices should be reviewed for applicable laws and requirements.

9. Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party resources, such as externally hosted fonts. These providers may process technical request information according to their own policies.

Any future integrations, such as sign-in providers, analytics or external career databases, should be documented here before being enabled for users.

10. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Career Compass AI develops and adds new features.

When the website's data practices change, the policy should be revised to accurately explain those changes and the date of the latest update.

11. Contact Us

If you have a question or suggestion about this Privacy Policy, you can use the website's contact page.

Go to Contact Us →

Please note that the current contact form is a demo and is not yet connected to a message-receiving service.
