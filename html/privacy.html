OUR PRIVACY APPROACH

What you should know

A simple overview of how information can be used while you explore Career Compass AI.

01 — INTRODUCTION

Your information matters

Career Compass AI is designed to help students and users explore career possibilities through assessments, recommendations, and career information.

This page explains how information may be handled while using the platform.

02 — INFORMATION

What information may be collected?

Depending on the features you use, the platform may handle information such as:

  • Name or profile information.
  • Email address.
  • Questionnaire responses.
  • Saved career preferences.
03 — USAGE

How information may be used

Information may help us provide a more personalized Career Compass AI experience.

  • Personalized career recommendations.
  • Career profile creation.
  • Saved career preferences.
  • Improving the platform experience.
04 — ASSESSMENT

Career assessment data

Answers provided through the career assessment may be used to create a career profile and suggest relevant career areas.

Recommendations are intended for exploration and guidance rather than a final career decision.

05 — LOCAL STORAGE

Browser storage

Some features may use browser local storage to remember information such as login state, saved careers, questionnaire results, and preferences.

This information is stored within the browser and device being used.

06 — SECURITY

Keeping information safe

We aim to handle information responsibly and maintain appropriate security for the platform.

However, no online service can guarantee complete security of information.

07 — THIRD PARTIES

External services

Future versions of Career Compass AI may use services such as authentication providers, databases, hosting platforms, or other tools.

Their own privacy policies may also apply.

08 — UPDATES

Changes to this policy

As Career Compass AI grows and new features are introduced, this Privacy Policy may be updated.

Future changes will be reflected on this page.

💡

A quick reminder

Career Compass AI is a career exploration and guidance platform. Avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive or private information while using the website.
